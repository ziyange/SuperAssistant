# PHASE-006 phase-plan snapshot

Goal: expand domains and adapters without weakening the H1/H2 control-plane
guarantees. Scope: curated domain templates, Adapter onboarding, safe Artifact
rendering, evidence/retention controls and a second dedicated Adapter.

The accepted second Adapter is DSH under ADR-007: one controlled process and
worktree per Attempt, platform-owned journal state, immutable policy and
brokered effects. Marketplace plugins, Team RBAC and automatic authority claims
remain out of scope.
